# Puzzle notes (spoilers)

Mr A is the Rev. Silas Carrow and Mr B is Mr Walter Dunmore. The story is a placeholder.

## How meetings work

**Dinner:** guests sit in places 1 to 6, with the Master (Ashworth) fixed in place 1. Neighbours meet. Mrs Ellery also introduces her left neighbour to her right neighbour.

**Bath:** there are three sessions (Morning, Afternoon, Evening), each with a Ball Room and an Octagon talk. A speaker can't attend talks in their own session. When free, they go to a talk they're keen on, and if they're keen on both, they choose the Ball Room. If they're keen on neither, they go to the Pump Room, and everyone at the Pump Room in that session meets. Miss Quill must speak in the morning, and Dunmore must be in the Ball Room.

Who is keen on whom (told to the player in the speakers' letters):

| Speaker | Keen on |
|---|---|
| Carrow | Dunmore, Hale |
| Dunmore | Rao, Pell |
| Hale | Quill |
| Quill | Dunmore, Hale |
| Rao | Pell, Quill |
| Pell | Hale, Quill |

## Dangerous pairs

| Event | Pair | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Dinner | Carrow + Dunmore | The engine. The whole library is destroyed. |
| Dinner | Bell + Dunmore | Radcliffe Camera fire, 1903 |
| Dinner | Ashworth + Dunmore | Old Schools Quadrangle tower collapses, 1899 |
| Dinner | Ellery + Fenwick | Duke Humfrey's bindings rot, 1912 |
| Bath | Carrow + Dunmore | The engine |
| Bath | Dunmore + Pell | Divinity School vault falls, 1896 |
| Bath | Pell + Quill | Clarendon Building crumbles, 1921 |

Every other pair that meets produces a harmless book, which serves as a clue.

## Solutions

Dinner (6 work): ABCFDE, ABECFD, ACBFDE, ACEBFD, AEBCFD, AECBFD. Dunmore always ends up beside Fenwick.

Bath (2 work), written as Morning Ball/Octagon, Afternoon, Evening:
- Hale/Quill, Dunmore/Carrow, Rao/Pell
- Hale/Quill, Rao/Pell, Dunmore/Carrow

The Bath trick: Carrow must share Dunmore's session. Hale in the Ball Room beside Quill pulls Pell away from Quill's talk, and Rao in the Ball Room beside Pell pulls Dunmore away from hers.
